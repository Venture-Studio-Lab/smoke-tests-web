Experiment privacy notice

This page explains the limited information collected by the concept-validation pages.

What is collected

Experiment telemetry may include the concept viewed, assigned price variant, page path, campaign/UTM fields, a random per-tab session identifier, and the event time. The experiment does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or browser fingerprinting.

If you reserve early access, the experiment also collects your email address, self-selected professional role, current substitute/tool, and usage frequency.

Why it is collected

The information is used to evaluate whether the concept has credible demand at the displayed price, distinguish professional intent from general curiosity, and contact people who asked to hear about early access if the concept moves forward.

What is deliberately not requested

No payment information, employer name, bank information, income, sensitive personal data, or confidential business information is requested.

Storage and access

Experiment data is stored in a dedicated Venture Studio Lab project, separate from unrelated products and systems. Public site visitors cannot read the experiment database through the site.

Retention

Data is kept only while reasonably necessary to evaluate the experiment and, for people who reserve, to manage the requested early-access follow-up. It should be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed for those purposes.

Your choice

Submitting a reservation is optional. No purchase or subscription is created and no payment is collected.
